# TD-03. What happens if a buyer's inventory is full at purchase?

- **Area:** Architecture
- **Decision:** TD-03. What happens if a buyer's inventory is full at purchase?
- **Default:** Reject the purchase
- **Status:** Open

## Options

- **A.** Reject the purchase; nothing changes.
- **B.** Deliver the item to a claim box the player empties later.

## Notes

B adds a new item location and a new screen. It also touches F3 in Product.

## Affects

HLD section 6 (market purchase) and the market module.
