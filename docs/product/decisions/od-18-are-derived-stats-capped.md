# OD-18. Are derived stats capped?

- **Area:** Progression
- **Decision:** OD-18. Are derived stats capped?
- **Default:** Yes, hard caps on rates
- **Status:** Open

## Question

Should rates such as crit rate, evasion, cooldown reduction, and penetration have hard caps?

## Default

Yes. Starting values: crit rate 60%, penetration 50%, cooldown reduction 40%. Hit chance never drops below 20% or rises above 99%. Tuned in playtests.

## Also to confirm

"CD" in the derived stat list is read as cooldown reduction, since crit damage is listed separately.

## Affects

The Progression and Combat pages.
