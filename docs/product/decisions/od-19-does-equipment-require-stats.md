# OD-19. Does equipment require stats?

- **Area:** Items
- **Decision:** OD-19. Does equipment require stats?
- **Default:** A. Minimum level and class only
- **Status:** Open

## Options

- **A.** A minimum level and class only.
- **B.** Also a minimum main stat, such as 40 STR for a sword.

## Affects

D3 and the Items page.
