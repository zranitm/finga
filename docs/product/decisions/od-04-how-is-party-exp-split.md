# OD-04. How is party EXP split?

- **Area:** Social
- **Decision:** OD-04. How is party EXP split?
- **Default:** A, plus a small party bonus
- **Status:** Open

## Options

- **A.** Equal split among members on the same map.
- **B.** By damage dealt.
- **C.** The killer gets most; the rest is shared.

## Affects

E3 and the Social page. Supports the "better together" pillar.
