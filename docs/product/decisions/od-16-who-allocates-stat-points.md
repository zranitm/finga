# OD-16. Who allocates stat points?

- **Area:** Progression
- **Decision:** OD-16. Who allocates stat points?
- **Default:** A. Players spend points at each level-up
- **Status:** Open

## Options

- **A.** Players spend points at each level-up.
- **B.** Stats grow automatically per class.
- **C.** Automatic by default, with optional manual allocation.

## Why the default

Letting players choose is what makes builds matter. It also moves C6 (stat allocation) from P2 into the MVP.

## Affects

C4, C6, and the Progression page.
