# OD-05. Who can pick up drops?

- **Area:** Items
- **Decision:** OD-05. Who can pick up drops?
- **Default:** B. Killer or party first, then anyone
- **Status:** Open

## Options

- **A.** Anyone.
- **B.** The killer or their party first for a short window, then anyone.
- **C.** Separate loot per player.

## Affects

D1, E3, and the Items page.
