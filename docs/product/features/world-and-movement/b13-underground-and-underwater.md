# B13. Underground and underwater

- **Feature:** B13. Underground and underwater
- **Group:** B. World and movement
- **Priority:** P1
- **Status:** Agreed

**Story:** As a player, I go down into a cave lit only by torches, or dive to a shrine sunk under the lake, and hunt there. These places look and feel unlike the land above, but fights work the same way.

## Acceptance

- **Two to start, both in the green region** (B10):
  - **The cave,** under the northern cliffs. It holds today's Cave's bats, boars and Vampire Bat, and the Thief's trainer, at the top of the region's levels.
  - **The sunken shrine,** under the region's lake, with new sea mobs and a sea boss.
  - The desert and the snow mountains get theirs, or none, when those regions are designed.
- **Open to everyone:**
  - Each is one of its region's hunting areas, with its own levels, camps, field boss (C10), quests and pouches (D1).
  - Everyone in the channel shares it, as they share the land.
  - It isn't a private copy. Party dungeons stay C7.
- **Getting in and out:**
  - The cave's mouth is an opening in the cliffs. The dive spot is a marked stone on the lake shore.
  - The talk key in its ring goes in, as at a gate, after a short load.
  - Inside, the way back is beside the arrival: the cave mouth's daylight, or a column of rising bubbles. It returns the player to the spot outside.
  - A player arrives in the channel with the same number as the one they left, so the players they saw go in are there. If that channel is full, they go to the least full one.
- **Waypoints:**
  - Each has a waypoint inside, by its entrance, found and teleported to like any other (B11).
  - A player who dies inside gets back up at that waypoint.
- **Underground:**
  - The only light comes from torches, mounted on the walls along every tunnel and standing round the camps and the boss.
  - Their flames flicker, and the light pools round them.
  - Between torches it's dim, never black, so mobs, players and warnings always show.
  - There's no sky. Above the walls there's only darkness.
- **Underwater:**
  - Players walk on the sea floor and breathe freely. There's no air bar.
  - Moving and fighting work as on land: speed, sprint, jumps, skills and reach.
  - It looks underwater:
    - blue-green light and fog;
    - light rippling on the ground and rays from above;
    - kelp swaying, coral and shells;
    - bubbles rising, and fish swimming by.
- **Sea mobs:**
  - A few new mobs, each using an attack mobs already have, and a boss. For example:
    - a crab that bites;
    - a jelly that floats and stings with poison;
    - an eel that charges.
  - Their names and models are picked from previews.
- **The rules don't change below.** Combat, statuses, the lock, pouches and the death penalty all work as on the land.
- **Map views** (B8):
  - While a player is inside, the minimap and the map window show the cave or the shrine.
  - The region's map marks the cave mouth and the dive spot, with their names and levels.
  - A party member inside shows at its entrance on the region's map. A member outside shows at the way back on the map inside.
- **Notices and chat:**
  - The boss's notices go to the players inside (C10).
  - The General tab reaches the players in the same place and channel (E1). Each place has its own.
- **Designed with the region:** their layouts, levels and names are designed with the green region's layout, from previews (B10).

## Not in this feature

- Swimming, or entering deep water from the land. Deep water still blocks (B10).
- An air bar, drowning, or swimming up and down.
- Darkness that hides mobs, or a light the player carries.
- Underground or underwater places in the desert and the snow mountains, which are decided with those regions.
- Private copies for a party (C7).

## Rules

World; B10 for regions; B11 for waypoints.
