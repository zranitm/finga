# B5. Towns

- **Feature:** B5. Towns
- **Group:** B. World and movement
- **Priority:** P0
- **Status:** Draft

**Story:** As a player, I can rest in towns where no mobs attack, respawn there, and reach the marketplace.

In a large region ([B10. Large regions](./b10-large-regions.md)) towns are areas inside it: no mob attacks and no one fights there, and a mob chasing a player gives up at the edge. A character who dies gets back up at the nearest waypoint or town in the region ([B11](./b11-sprint-and-waypoints.md)).

## Acceptance

- No mobs appear in towns and no combat happens there.
- Characters who die respawn in the town linked to their map.
- The marketplace is reachable from towns, once F3 exists.
