# B1. Maps

- **Feature:** B1. Maps
- **Group:** B. World and movement
- **Priority:** P0
- **Status:** Draft

**Story:** As a player, I explore side-scrolling maps with ground, platforms, ropes, and ladders.

## Acceptance

- Maps contain ground, walls, one-way platforms, ropes and ladders, and spots where mobs appear.
- Maps behave the same for every player.
- From wherever a player arrives on a map, they can walk, jump, climb and drop to every portal, every platform and every spot where mobs appear, and nowhere traps them.
- Entering a map shows its name and, on a hunting map, its mobs' level range.
