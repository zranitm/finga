# B1. Maps

- **Feature:** B1. Maps
- **Group:** B. World and movement
- **Priority:** P0
- **Status:** Draft

**Story:** As a player, I explore 3D maps of open ground and hills, with rocks, trees, walls, and houses to walk round or jump onto.

## Acceptance

- Maps contain ground and hills, solids such as rocks, trees, walls, and houses, portals, and spots where mobs appear.
- Maps behave the same for every player.
- From wherever a player arrives on a map, they can walk and jump to every portal, every NPC, and every spot where mobs appear, and nowhere traps them.
- This holds at every move speed. Moving faster lowers a jump slightly, so anything a player has to jump onto is low enough for the fastest character.
- Entering a map shows its name and, on a hunting map, its mobs' level range.
