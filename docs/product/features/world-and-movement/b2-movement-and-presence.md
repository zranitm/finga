# B2. Movement and presence

- **Feature:** B2. Movement and presence
- **Group:** B. World and movement
- **Priority:** P0
- **Status:** Draft

**Story:** As a player, I can walk, jump, climb, and drop through platforms, and I see other players on the same map moving smoothly.

## Acceptance

- Walk, jump, climb ropes and ladders, and drop down through one-way platforms.
- While using a skill, walk and climb at half speed, facing the same way (C11).
- Players on the same map see each other join, leave, and move smoothly, with name labels.
- Players can't move faster than allowed, leave the map, or pass through walls or mobs (C12).
