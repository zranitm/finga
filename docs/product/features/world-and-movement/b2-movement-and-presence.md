# B2. Movement and presence

- **Feature:** B2. Movement and presence
- **Group:** B. World and movement
- **Priority:** P0
- **Status:** Draft

**Story:** As a player, I can walk and jump, and I see other players on the same map moving smoothly.

## Acceptance

- Walk in eight directions, over hills and round solids, and jump onto low rocks and ledges.
- Using a basic attack or a skill never stops movement (C11).
- Players on the same map see each other join, leave, and move smoothly, with name labels.
- Players can't move faster than allowed, leave the map, or pass through walls or mobs (C12).
