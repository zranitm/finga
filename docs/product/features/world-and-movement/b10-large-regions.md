# B10. Large regions

- **Feature:** B10. Large regions
- **Group:** B. World and movement
- **Priority:** P0
- **Status:** Agreed

**Story:** As a player, I explore a few big, open regions, as in Blade & Soul, instead of many small maps. I walk from a town across meadows and hills to a camp on a ridge I could see from far off, meeting other players on the way, with no loading until I cross into the next region.

## Acceptance

- **Three regions,** each about 1 km across: a green region for levels 1–12, a desert for 12–22, and snow mountains for 22–30 with the endgame keep (World).
- **Seamless inside:** a region is one place. Walking, sprinting (B11) and fighting cross it with no loading screen. Only a gate to another region, a teleport to another region, or dying in a region not yet built loads.
- **Areas:**
  - Each region is divided into about five named areas, each with its mob levels and its field boss.
  - Walking into an area shows its name and, outside towns, its level range, as entering a map does today.
- **Towns:**
  - A region has a main town and smaller ones along its roads.
  - Inside a town no mob attacks and no one fights. A mob chasing a player gives up at the town's edge.
- **Camps:** mobs live in camps across each area: a few of one kind, or a normal mob with its elite. They come back after they're beaten, as today.
- **Field bosses** (C10) belong to an area: kills in the area count toward its boss, and the area's players see its bar and share its rewards.
- **Getting around:**
  - Roads link towns and areas. Rivers have bridges and fords, and mountains are crossed by passes.
  - Waypoints along the way are found by walking past them (B11).
  - Sprint, out of combat, covers the distances (B11).
- **Region edges:** mountains and cliffs ring each region. A gate in a pass leads to the next region.
- **What a player sees:**
  - The land, far off: mountains, ridges and the shapes of distant woods.
  - Other players, mobs and drops within about 100 m.
  - The camera zooms out for the view (B12).
- **Crowds:**
  - Each region runs in channels of up to 150 players (B4).
  - A player joins the least busy channel, with their party if there's room.
  - A full region opens another channel. A spare one with no one in it closes.
- **Region chat:** the General tab reaches everyone in the same region and channel (E1).
- **Dying** brings the player back at the nearest waypoint or town in the region, with the usual death penalty (C3).
- **Building it:** regions are generated from a plan that names their areas, mountains, rivers, roads, towns and camps (World). Towns, ruins and landmarks are placed by hand on top.
- **Rollout:**
  - The green region comes first and replaces Town, Forest and Cave.
  - The other maps stay, behind a gate at the green region's edge, until the desert and snow regions replace them.
  - A character on a retired map starts at the green region's main town.
- **Fairness:** the server decides every move, hit, count and channel, as today.

## Not in this feature

- Mounts, gliding and swimming. Deep water can't be entered.
- Dungeons and instanced areas.
- Weather and day and night.
- One seamless world with no loads between regions.

## Rules

World; B11 for sprint and waypoints; B12 for the camera.
