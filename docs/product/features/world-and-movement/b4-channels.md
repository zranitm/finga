# B4. Channels

- **Feature:** B4. Channels
- **Group:** B. World and movement
- **Priority:** P1
- **Status:** Draft

**Story:** As a player, I can pick a channel and switch channels, so crowded maps stay playable.

For large regions ([B10. Large regions](./b10-large-regions.md)): each region runs in channels of up to 150 players. A player joins the least full one, with their party if there's room; a full region opens another, and a spare one left empty closes. The map window shows the channel and switches it, with a 3-second channel cancelled as a teleport is.

## Acceptance

- Each map exists in several independent channels.
- Players see only those in the same map and channel.
- Switching channels keeps the character's state and party.
