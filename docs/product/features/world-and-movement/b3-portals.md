# B3. Portals

- **Feature:** B3. Portals
- **Group:** B. World and movement
- **Priority:** P0
- **Status:** Draft

**Story:** As a player, I can step into a portal and press the talk key to travel to a connected map.

Inside a large region there are no portals ([B10. Large regions](./b10-large-regions.md)). A gate at a region's edge works as a portal does, to the next region. Portals stay on the old maps until those are retired.

## Acceptance

- A portal is a glowing ring on the ground, labelled with the map it leads to.
- The talk key, pressed while standing in a portal, takes the character to the linked spot on the connected map.
- Nothing is lost on the way: HP, items, currency, and party stay the same.
