# B12. Camera zoom

- **Feature:** B12. Camera zoom
- **Group:** B. World and movement
- **Priority:** P1
- **Status:** Agreed

**Story:** As a player, I zoom the camera out to take in a big region, or in for a close fight, and it still faces north, so my keys never change.

## Acceptance

- **Zoom:** the mouse wheel, or the zoom keys (= and − by default, rebindable in B6), moves the camera between today's close view and about 40 m away.
- **The angle lowers as it pulls back,** from today's 50° down to about 35°, so a zoomed-out view reaches far across the land toward the horizon.
- **Always north-facing:** the arrow keys still walk up for north. Aiming, the lock and every key work as before.
- **What shows far off** (B10): the land and its shapes to the region's edge. Players, mobs and drops show within about 100 m.
- **Kept:** the zoom is saved with the account's settings.
- **In a PvP match** (I1) the zoom works the same.

## Not in this feature

- Turning the camera.
- A first-person view.

## Rules

Screens and controls.
