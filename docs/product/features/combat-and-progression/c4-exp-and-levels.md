# C4. EXP and levels

- **Decisions:** OD-13
- **Feature:** C4. EXP and levels
- **Group:** C. Combat and progression
- **Priority:** P0
- **Status:** Draft

**Story:** As a player, I gain EXP from defeating mobs and level up, getting stronger.

## Acceptance

- Each mob type gives a set amount of EXP.
- Reaching the threshold levels the character up and gives 5 stat points to spend (C6).
- The EXP bar and level are always visible.
- EXP gained and lost, and level-ups, show in the combat channel (E5).
