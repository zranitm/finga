# C17. A fuller skill roster

- **Feature:** C17. A fuller skill roster
- **Group:** C. Combat and progression
- **Priority:** P1
- **Status:** Agreed

**Story:** As a player, my class has more than a couple of attacks. I can close in on a mob, get out of trouble, control a pack, and buff myself or my party, so each fight plays out differently and my class feels like its own.

## Acceptance

- **22 new skills:** five for each first class, plus two for the Beginner, which every class keeps after promotion. They replace the "placeholder" note on each class's first two skills, which stay as they are.
- **Unlocked by level:** each new skill takes its first point from the level the table gives, so a class grows into its kit up to the level cap of 30.
- **Six keys, as now:** players choose which six actives to carry. Since B6's key bindings the bar has eight. Skill chains (C18) put follow-ups on their root's key, so six keys carry most of a class's kit.

- **Approach skills** need a lock. With none, they go the way the player faces, as far as they reach.
- **Escape skills and Blink give a dodge:** for a moment, mobs' attacks pass through the player, who shows faded. It's a skill on a cooldown, not a dash key, so the no-dash rule stands for plain movement.
- **Moving skills** stop at walls, rocks, and the map's edge, and an approach stops at its mob.
- **Control on mobs** (now part of C19, Abnormal statuses, which also covers mobs' statuses on players):
  - **Stun and freeze:** the mob can't move or attack.
  - **Pin:** the mob can't move, but still attacks what it reaches.
  - **Knockdown:** as three quick hits do today.
  - **Knockback:** the mob is pushed away.
  - **Taunt:** the mob attacks the taunter.
  - **Losing sight:** the mob stops chasing.
  - Each shows on the mob: stars, ice, roots, and so on.
  - Elites and bosses shrug off stuns, pins, knockdowns, and knockbacks, as they shrug off knockdowns today. Taunt and losing sight still work on them.
- **Party buffs** (War Cry) reach party members within 8 m of the Warrior.
- **Poison** hurts once a second for its 4 s and shows green on the mob. Its damage counts as the Thief's, for kills and EXP.
- **Barrier's shield** shows as a bubble round the Magician and a pale bar over their HP. Damage takes the shield first.
- **Every new skill** has its icon (C16), costs 2 or 3 energy, has a cooldown, and evolves once at 10 points into a stronger form.
- **Fairness:** the server moves players and mobs, applies every effect, and decides every hit, as for today's skills.

## Not in this feature

- Skill traits, and the skill window's new layout: [C20. Skill traits](./c20-skill-traits.md).
- More than six keys.
- Second promotions and their skills.
- Effects on other players: no player-versus-player.
