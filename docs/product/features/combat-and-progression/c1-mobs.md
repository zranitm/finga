# C1. Mobs

- **Feature:** C1. Mobs
- **Group:** C. Combat and progression
- **Priority:** P0
- **Status:** Draft

**Story:** As a player, I fight mobs that appear, move around, and attack me.

## Acceptance

- Mobs appear at set spots up to a maximum per map and patrol. Players can't walk through them, and they hurt players with their attacks, not by touch (C12).
- Defeated mobs come back after a while.
- Every player on the map sees the same mobs in the same places.
- An elite is bigger and slower than its map's normal mob, takes about 2.5 times the hits, and gives more EXP and better drops. Its HP bar has a gold frame.
- Each hunting map also has a field boss, which appears after enough kills there (C10).
