# C3. HP, death, and respawn

- **Decisions:** OD-06
- **Feature:** C3. HP, death, and respawn
- **Group:** C. Combat and progression
- **Priority:** P0
- **Status:** Draft

**Story:** As a player, I lose HP when hit; at zero I die and respawn in town.

## Acceptance

- HP is always visible and drops when the character is hit.
- At zero HP the character dies, then respawns in the linked town.
- The death penalty is applied as defined.

Systems → Combat; Systems → Progression
