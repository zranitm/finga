# C8. Class promotion

- **Feature:** C8. Class promotion
- **Group:** C. Combat and progression
- **Priority:** P1
- **Status:** Draft

**Story:** As a Beginner, I choose my class when I'm ready, by meeting its conditions and talking to its trainer.

## Acceptance

- **Beginners:** every new character is a Beginner, with the Beginner's attack, skills, and starting gear.
- **Trainers:**
  - Each first class (Warrior, Thief, Bowman, Magician) has a trainer NPC, in a town or a hunting map (H1).
  - Talking to a trainer shows the class, how it fights, and each promotion condition, marked met or not met. The conditions are level, stats, skill proficiency, and a finished quest.
- **Promoting:**
  - When every condition is met, the trainer offers the promotion. The player confirms it in a dialog that says it's permanent.
  - The promotion takes effect at once: the new basic attack, base values, and class skills.
  - Beginner skills and their points, stat and skill points, level, EXP, items, and party all stay.
  - Everyone on the map sees the character's new class, and the party panel shows it.
  - The player may promote at any trainer whose conditions they meet; nothing else limits the choice.
- **Staying a Beginner:** a Beginner who doesn't promote keeps playing and levelling, with no cap.
- **Permanence:** a promotion can't be undone.

## Not in this feature

- Second promotions (later).
- Changing class after a promotion.
