# A1. Sign up and log in

- **Decisions:** OD-08
- **Feature:** A1. Sign up and log in
- **Group:** A. Account and character
- **Priority:** P0
- **Status:** Draft

**Story:** As a player, I can create an account and log in, so my progress is kept.

## Acceptance

- Sign up with a unique username and a password.
- Log in to continue where I left off.
- Too many wrong passwords in a row temporarily blocks further attempts.
