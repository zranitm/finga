# I2. Arena: 1v1 and 3v3

- **Feature:** I2. Arena: 1v1 and 3v3
- **Group:** I. PvP
- **Priority:** P1
- **Status:** Agreed

**Story:** As a player, I fight one opponent, or two teammates and I fight three, in a small walled arena. The first side to win two rounds wins.

## Acceptance

- **The arena:** a round, walled ring about 36 m across, with four pillars to break a straight line. Each side starts at its own end.
- **A match is best of three rounds.** The first side to win two rounds wins.
- **Before the first round:** 10 seconds on the player's own side, to look at the opponents and change the arena build.
- **A round:**
  - A 3-second countdown, when no one can act, then the fight.
  - Everyone starts at full HP, full energy and no cooldowns, with no buffs or statuses.
  - **1v1:** the round ends when one fighter goes down, or after 60 seconds.
  - **3v3:** the round ends when one whole team is down, or after 90 seconds. A downed player stays down and watches until the round ends.
  - **On time:** the side with the larger share of its total HP left wins the round. Each fighter's share counts, out of their own maximum.
  - **An even round** counts for neither side. A match is over after five rounds at most; if no side has won two by then, it's a draw.
  - Between rounds: 5 seconds showing who won the round, when the build can change.
- **The match bar,** at the top centre: the mode, a pip for each round won by each side, and the round's time left.
- **3v3:**
  - Teammates show as party frames on the left, with their HP and statuses. The three enemies show as small frames under the match bar.
  - Parties of two or three queue together. A party of two gets one more teammate: a player near their rating, or a bot.
- **The result screen:** Victory, Defeat or Draw, each round's winner, and the rating change and rank (I4). The player is taken back after 10 seconds, or at once with Return.

## Not in this feature

- Reviving teammates.
- Tag team, with one fighter per side at a time.
- More arenas to choose from.

## Rules

I1 for everything every match shares; I4 for rating.
