# I3. Battlefield: 15v15

- **Feature:** I3. Battlefield: 15v15
- **Group:** I. PvP
- **Priority:** P1
- **Status:** Agreed

**Story:** As a player, I join a 15-a-side battle on a big map, with my party or alone. Our side wins by holding capture points longer than theirs. Bots fill every place no player takes, so I can play it any time.

## Acceptance

- **The battlefield:** a map about 140 m by 90 m, with a base at each end and three capture points between them: west, middle and east. Hills and ruins break it up.
- **Teams:** two sides of 15. Parties of up to four queue together and stay on one side. Bots fill every place left after 15 seconds.
- **The start:** 20 seconds in the base, behind its closed gate, then the gates open.
- **Capture points:**
  - A point is a circle 10 m across, shown on the ground, the minimap and the map window.
  - Standing in a point that no enemy stands in turns it to your side in 8 seconds. Each extra teammate in it makes that quicker, up to twice as fast.
  - A point the other side holds must first be turned back to no one's, then to yours, so taking it from them takes twice as long.
  - While both sides stand in a point, it doesn't move.
- **Score:**
  - Each point a side holds gives it 1 score a second.
  - Downing an enemy gives the downer's side 5.
  - The first side to 1,000 wins. Otherwise the side with more after 12 minutes wins; an even score is a draw.
- **Going down:** a downed player gets back up in their base after 10 seconds, at full HP, with their cooldowns still running.
- **The base:**
  - The other side can't enter it.
  - Standing in it gives back 10% of HP a second.
- **The match bar,** at the top centre: both sides' scores, the three points in their holders' colours, each with a ring that fills as it turns, and the time left.
- **Frames:** the player's party shows as party frames, as in the world. There are no enemy frames; the lock's target window shows the locked enemy.
- **A player who leaves** is replaced by a bot. A lost connection holds their place for 20 seconds first (I1).
- **No rating:** 15v15 gives no rating, only the result. Guild war will rate guilds later.
- **The result screen:** Victory, Defeat or Draw, both scores, and the player's own downs and points taken. The player is taken back after 10 seconds, or at once with Return.

## Not in this feature

- Guilds, guild war and its rewards.
- Joining a battle already under way, even in a bot's place. Coming back to one's own held place after a lost connection (I1) isn't that.
- Siege objects, mounts, or more than one battlefield.

## Rules

I1 for everything every match shares.
