# F2. NPC shop

- **Feature:** F2. NPC shop
- **Group:** F. Economy
- **Priority:** P1
- **Status:** Draft

**Story:** As a player, I can buy potions and sell unwanted items to an NPC in town.

## Acceptance

- Prices are fixed, and an NPC always buys for less than it sells.
- A purchase or sale happens fully or not at all: currency and item change together.
