# F1. Currency wallet

- **Feature:** F1. Currency wallet
- **Group:** F. Economy
- **Priority:** P1
- **Status:** Draft

**Story:** As a player, I earn currency from drops and spend it in shops and on the marketplace.

## Acceptance

- The balance is always visible and never goes below zero.
- Currency is never created or lost by mistake.
- Every change can be traced to its cause: a drop, a shop, a trade, or a fee.
