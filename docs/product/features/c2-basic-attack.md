# C2. Basic attack

- **Feature:** C2. Basic attack
- **Group:** C. Combat and progression
- **Priority:** P0
- **Status:** Draft

**Story:** As a player, I can attack mobs and see the damage I deal.

## Acceptance

- Attacks hit mobs within range in the direction the character faces.
- Damage depends on the attacker's and the target's stats.
- Damage numbers appear above the target.
- Every player sees the same result, and damage can't be faked.
