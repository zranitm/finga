# D5. Item integrity

- **Feature:** D5. Item integrity
- **Group:** D. Items and inventory
- **Priority:** P0
- **Status:** Draft

**Story:** As a player, I trust that items can't be duplicated or lost, so the economy stays fair.

## Acceptance

- Every piece of equipment can be told apart from an identical-looking one.
- Items are never duplicated or lost by mistake, even if the game disconnects mid-action.
- Every item's history can be traced: where it came from and who has held it.
