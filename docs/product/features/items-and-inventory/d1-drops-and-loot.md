# D1. Drops and loot

- **Decisions:** OD-05
- **Feature:** D1. Drops and loot
- **Group:** D. Items and inventory
- **Priority:** P0
- **Status:** Draft

**Story:** As a player, I pick up currency and items that defeated mobs drop.

## Acceptance

- Defeated mobs drop items based on their drop list, and currency once F1 exists.
- Drops lie on the ground, can be picked up with a key, and vanish after a while.
- With a full inventory, a pickup fails and the drop stays on the ground.
