# D2. Inventory

- **Feature:** D2. Inventory
- **Group:** D. Items and inventory
- **Priority:** P0
- **Status:** Draft

**Story:** As a player, I keep my items in an inventory that stays with my character.

## Acceptance

- Tabs by item type (Equipment, Use, Etc), each with a fixed number of slots.
- Use and Etc items stack.
- Items can be discarded, one at a time or several at once. In select mode the player ticks items in the open tab, and one confirm discards them all. The confirm gives the count and names any item of four stars or more (the user's call of 2026-11-12).
- The inventory is unchanged after logging out and back in.
