# D9. Getting outfits

- **Feature:** D9. Getting outfits
- **Group:** D. Items and inventory
- **Priority:** P1
- **Status:** Agreed

**Story:** As a player, I collect outfit pieces by playing: rare drops from field bosses, PvP ranks, quests, and first-time feats.

## Acceptance

- **Field bosses** (C10):
  - Each boss drops one or two pieces themed to it, rarely, on each player's own roll.
  - The undead bosses drop skeleton outfits.
  - These pieces drop as items (D8), so they can be traded once trading exists.
- **PvP ranks** (I4):
  - The first time a character reaches Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond or Master, in 1v1 or 3v3, the account gets that rank's piece.
  - Falling back and climbing again gives nothing more.
- **Quests** (H2): some quests give a piece on hand-in, listed with their other rewards.
- **Feats:**
  - A first-time deed gives a piece, once per account.
  - Examples: reaching level 10 or 30, a first field boss beaten, a first PvP win, a promotion.
  - The wardrobe names the feat under its piece.
- **Ranks, quests and feats** put the piece straight in the wardrobe. It can't be traded.
- **Later:**
  - An outfit vendor in town, once gold and NPC shops exist (F1, F2).
  - Gacha or a paid shop, if either is ever decided (G1).
  - Nothing paid is in this feature.
- **The first catalogue,** about 45 pieces:
  - about 19 outfits: the five adventurers' outfits in three colourways each, and four skeleton outfits;
  - about 10 headwear;
  - 4 face pieces made for the game;
  - about 12 weapon looks.
  - Its colourways, and which source gives which piece, are designed with the user from previews first.

## Not in this feature

- The outfit vendor, trading, gacha and paid shops.
- Seasons and event outfits.
- An achievements window: feats show only in the wardrobe.

## Rules

Systems → Items → Outfits; D8 for the wardrobe.
