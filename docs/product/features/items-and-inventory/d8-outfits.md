# D8. Outfits and wardrobe

- **Feature:** D8. Outfits and wardrobe
- **Group:** D. Items and inventory
- **Priority:** P1
- **Status:** Agreed

**Story:** As a player, I dress my character in outfits I've collected, as in Blade & Soul: a costume, headwear, a face piece, a weapon look and wings, whatever gear I wear for stats. Other players see it.

## Acceptance

- **Gear never shows.** What a character wears for stats (D3) doesn't change how it looks. It looks like its class, or wears the pieces the player picks.
- **Five slots:**
  - **Outfit:** the whole body, its cape included.
  - **Headwear:** a hat, helmet or hood. It can be hidden.
  - **Face:** a mask, glasses, an eye patch or goggles.
  - **Weapon look:** how the class's weapon looks. A weapon look fits the classes whose weapon is its kind: one-handed (Beginner, Warrior), knife (Thief), crossbow (Bowman) or staff (Magician).
  - **Wings:** on the back. While wings are worn, the outfit's cape hides, so the two never clip.
  - Any slot can stay on the class's own look. A class has no wings of its own.
- **Wings move by themselves:**
  - a slow flutter standing, faster when walking and faster still sprinting;
  - spread wide in a jump;
  - folded during an attack or skill, so they never hide the action.
  - Prestige wings glow softly.
- **Any class** can wear any outfit, headwear, face piece and wings.
- **The wardrobe** (O):
  - One per account. A piece collected by one character can be worn by all the account's characters.
  - It lists every piece in the game, slot by slot. Collected pieces are lit. The rest are greyed, with how to get them.
  - Picking a piece tries it on the character shown in the window, collected or not. Dragging turns the character.
  - Wear puts on the collected picks. Reset goes back to what's worn.
  - A count shows how many of all the pieces are collected.
- **Changing** works anywhere, at once, while alive and out of combat. In a PvP match, that's before a round or between rounds.
- **Kept** per character, through logouts, map changes and deaths.
- **Everyone sees it:** other players see a character's pieces, in the world and in PvP matches. Frames, the lock ring and team colours are unchanged.
- **Outfit items:**
  - A piece from a drop comes as an item in the Etc tab. It can be kept, and traded once trading exists (F3, F4).
  - Its card shows the piece, with Preview and Add to wardrobe.
  - Adding asks first, since an added piece can't be traded. Then the item is gone and the piece is in the wardrobe.
  - An item for a piece already collected can't be added. It stays an item, to trade.
- **Pieces from PvP ranks, quests and feats** go straight into the wardrobe, and can't be traded (D9).
- **A new piece** shows a notice: "New in your wardrobe: <name>".
- **Promotion:** a weapon look the new class can't use goes back to the class's own.
- **The character window** shows the character as it looks, in 3D, where its figure was empty.
- **Fairness:** looks never change stats, hits or reach. The server checks that every piece worn is collected.

## Not in this feature

- Dyeing a piece any colour.
- Gear that shows on the character.
- Hair, face and colours chosen at creation (A4).
- Off-hand looks, such as shields and books.
- Hiding other players' outfits.
- Flying, gliding or falling slowly: wings are looks only.

## Rules

Systems → Items → Outfits; D9 for where pieces come from.
