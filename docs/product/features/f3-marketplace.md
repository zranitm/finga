# F3. Marketplace

- **Decisions:** OD-07, OD-10
- **Feature:** F3. Marketplace
- **Group:** F. Economy
- **Priority:** P1
- **Status:** Draft

**Story:** As a player, I can list items for sale and buy items from other players.

## Acceptance

- List an item at a price; the item leaves the seller's inventory while listed.
- Browse and search listings by name, type, and price.
- A purchase happens fully or not at all: the buyer pays, the seller receives the price minus the fee, and the buyer gets the item.
- Only one buyer can buy a given listing.
- Proceeds reach the seller even when offline.
- Cancelling a listing returns the item; unsold listings expire and the item returns.
