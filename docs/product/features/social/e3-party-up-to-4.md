# E3. Party (up to 4)

- **Decisions:** OD-04, OD-05
- **Feature:** E3. Party (up to 4)
- **Group:** E. Social
- **Priority:** P0
- **Status:** Draft

**Story:** As a player, I can team up with up to 3 others to hunt together and share rewards.

## Acceptance

- Create a party, invite, accept or decline, leave, kick, and hand over leadership.
- See each member's level, HP, and current map.
- Party chat.
- Members on the same map share EXP from kills.
- The party stays together across maps and channels, and disbands when the last member leaves.
