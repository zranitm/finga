# H1. NPCs

- **Feature:** H1. NPCs
- **Group:** H. NPCs and quests
- **Priority:** P1
- **Status:** Draft

**Story:** As a player, I can talk to NPCs placed around the world, for promotions, quests, and later shops.

## Acceptance

- NPCs stand at set places in towns and hunting maps, the same for everyone. They can't be attacked and don't block anyone.
- Standing within reach and pressing Talk (Z in Classic, E in WASD) opens the NPC's dialog with what it offers. Talk does nothing when no NPC is in reach.
- The dialog closes when the player walks out of reach, dies, or changes maps.
- A marker above an NPC shows when it has something for this player: a quest to give, a quest to hand in, or a promotion ready (proposal).
