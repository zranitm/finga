# World

## Structure

- **The world is three large regions,** each about 1 km across, in the manner of Blade & Soul (B10). A region is seamless: players walk, sprint and fight across it with no loading. A short load comes only between regions.
- **Areas:** each region is divided into named areas, each with its own mobs, level range and field boss. Higher ranges sit further from the region's first town.
- **Towns:** safe areas inside a region. No mobs and no combat; NPCs, shops and, later, the marketplace.
- **Camps:** the places in an area where mobs live, and how many at most.
- **Waypoints:** found by walking past them; players teleport between the ones they've found, and get back up at the nearest one after dying (B11).
- **Gates:** a mountain pass at a region's edge leads to the next region.
- **Underground and underwater:** a cave or a sunken place entered from a region, through a cave mouth or a dive spot, with a short load. Each is one of its region's areas (B13).
- **Channels:** each region runs in channels, independent copies, so a crowd never fills one (B4). Players see only those in their own region and channel, and only within view.

## What a region contains

- Rolling ground, mountains along its edges, ridges and cliffs, rivers with bridges and fords, lakes, and roads linking its towns and areas.
- Trees, rocks, ruins and houses to walk round, and low ones to jump onto. Grass, earth, rock, sand and snow, painted by area, height and slope.
- Set pieces placed by hand: towns, ruins, camps and landmarks.
- Regions are generated from a plan: their areas, mountains, rivers, roads, towns and camps. A generator shapes the ground and scatters the trees and rocks, so a region can be reworked quickly.

## Rules

- Regions behave the same for every player: a rock one player stands on is there for everyone.
- Changing regions or channels never loses HP, items, currency or party.
- A character who dies gets back up at the nearest waypoint or town in their region. See Systems → Combat.

## The world

| Region | Levels | Character |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Green region** (Willowmere) | 1–12 | Meadows, woods, a mushroom glade, a lake and the northern cliffs; the starting town in the south and a small town in the middle. Below it: a cave under the cliffs, lit by torches, and a shrine sunk under the lake |
| **Desert** (Sunreach) | 12–22 | A shaded gorge of giant mushrooms in the south-west, terraces above it, an oasis town in the middle, dunes to the east and a sandstone mesa to the north. Below it: a fungus cave under the gorge and a temple buried under the mesa |
| **Snow mountains** (Hoarfell) | 22–30 | A mountain climbed in terraces: a village and pine trails at its foot, cliffs of blue ice, a glacier with a lodge at the top of the climb, and the Fallen Keep on the summit as the endgame: a challenge for characters at the level cap |

- **Areas:** about five a region, each a few levels wide, each with its field boss.
- **Mobs:** each type has one level, so a region's areas take the types whose levels fit them. Where none fit, new ones are made: the green region adds twelve for levels 5 to 14, a normal mob, an elite and a boss for each of four areas. Once a region replaces the old maps that held a mob's level, the mob can move: the desert takes the mushroom woods' and the desert maps' mobs three or four levels down, and the snow mobs move down to follow on.
- **Region names and their layouts** are designed one region at a time, from the generator's previews. The green region's: [The green region](../tech/green-region.md). The desert's: [The desert](../tech/desert-region.md). The snow region's: [The snow region](../tech/snow-region.md).
- **Underground and underwater** (B13): the green region has two to start. The cave takes today's Cave's mobs, its Vampire Bat and the Thief's trainer. The sunken shrine has new sea mobs. Inside, players walk and fight as on land. The desert has two: a fungus cave with the old Spore Hollow's mobs, under the gorge, and a buried temple with the old Sunken Ruins', under the mesa. The snow region has none: all four of its areas are on the mountain.

## The small maps

The regions replaced the 14 small maps one region at a time (B10): the green region replaced Town, Forest and Cave; the desert, the mushroom woods and the desert maps; the snow region, the last five: Frost Trail, Icefall, Glacier Peak, the Fallen Keep and the Crossroads. A character saved on a retired map starts at Willowmere's start. Only the PvP maps are maps now, and a region's gate leads to the next region's.
