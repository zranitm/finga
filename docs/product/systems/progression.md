# Progression

## Characters

- Four classes at the start: **Warrior**, **Thief**, **Bowman**, **Magician**. A class is chosen at character creation and doesn't change.
- How the classes differ in the MVP: OD-15 (default: each class has its own basic attack style and stat growth; skills come later).
- A character has a level, EXP, HP, MP, and stats.
- Five primary stats: **STR**, **DEX**, **INT**, **AGI**, **VIT**. Each class gets its attack power from its main stat, and VIT adds survivability for everyone.
- Class distributions (proposal): Warrior STR main, VIT secondary; Thief AGI main, DEX secondary; Bowman DEX main, AGI secondary; Magician INT main, VIT secondary. Putting everything into the main stat gives the most damage but leaves the character fragile.
- Derived stats, raised by primary stats and equipment: accuracy, evasion, crit rate, crit damage, penetration, cooldown reduction, plus max HP, max MP, attack power, and defense.
- Open: who allocates points (OD-16), resets (OD-17), caps (OD-18), and equipment requirements (OD-19).

## Leveling

- EXP comes from defeating mobs. In a party, EXP is shared (see Social).
- Reaching the EXP threshold raises the level by one. Extra EXP carries over to the next level.
- Each level-up raises stats by a fixed amount and fully restores HP and MP (proposal).
- The EXP needed per level grows with each level. The exact curve is still to define; target (proposal): about 10 hours of play to reach the MVP level cap.
- Level cap in the MVP: OD-13 (default 30).

## Death

- Death penalty: OD-06 (default: lose a small percentage of current EXP, outside towns only).
- EXP never drops below the start of the current level, so a character can never lose a level.
