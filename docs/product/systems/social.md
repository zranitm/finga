# Social

## Chat

- **Map chat (P0):** everyone in the same map and channel.
- **Party chat (P0):** members of the player's party, anywhere.
- **Whispers (P1):** one player, by name, anywhere.
- **Combat channel (P1):** not players talking but what happens to you while you play: EXP gained and lost, items you collect, currency once F1 exists, level-ups, drops waiting for room, and which party member got each drop. Only you see your lines.
- The chat box has three tabs: **General** (everything players say, map and party), **Party** (party chat only), and **Combat** (the combat channel).
- A player who sends messages too fast is muted for a short time.

## Party

- Up to 4 members, with one leader.
- The leader invites; the invited player accepts or declines.
- Any member can leave. The leader can kick members and hand over leadership. If the leader leaves, leadership passes to another member.
- Members see each other's level, HP, and current map.
- A member who logs out stays in the party and shows as offline (proposal).
- The party stays together across maps and channels, and disbands when the last member leaves.
- EXP sharing: open (default: equal split among members on the same map, plus a small party bonus).
- Loot: round-robin.
  - The living members on the same map as the kill take turns, one drop each. A member who is dead when the mob dies is skipped, and the drop goes to the next living member.
  - The turn moves on with every drop and carries over from one kill to the next. A kill with three drops goes to three members in turn.
  - The order is the order members joined the party; when someone joins or leaves, the turn carries on with the next member still there (proposal).
  - A drop waits for the member whose turn it was, even if their inventory is full; it isn't passed on (proposal).
  - Every member on the map sees who got what in their combat channel.
- A field boss's drops don't take turns: each player gets their own roll (Combat → Field bosses).
- Currency (once F1 exists) doesn't take turns: each currency drop is split evenly among the same living members on the map. What doesn't divide evenly goes to the killer (proposal).
