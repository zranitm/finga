# Items

## Item types

These are also the inventory tabs.

- **Equipment:** weapons and armor. Each piece is one of a kind. Raises stats and may need a minimum level.
- **Use:** consumables such as potions. Stackable.
- **Etc:** materials and mob drops with no direct use, sold to shops or other players. Stackable.

## Rarity

- OD-14 (default: no rarity tiers in the MVP).

## Drops

- Each mob type has a drop list: which items and currency it can drop, and the chance of each.
- Drops land on the ground, can be picked up, and vanish after a while.
- Who can pick up a drop: OD-05 (default: the killer or their party first for a short window, then anyone).

## Inventory

- Each tab has a fixed number of slots. Stacks have a maximum size.
- With a full inventory, a pickup fails and the drop stays on the ground.
- Items can be dropped on the ground or discarded.

## Integrity

- Every piece of equipment can be told apart from an identical-looking one.
- Items are never duplicated or lost by mistake, even if the game disconnects mid-action.
- Every item's history can be traced: where it came from and who has held it.
