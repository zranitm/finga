# Models

Every model here is CC0 1.0, in the public domain, or our own work, made by a script in `web/scripts/` whose row names it. `npm run models` makes each from its copy in `web/models-src/`: it keeps the clips a look plays and the gear a look shows, resamples the clips, and quantizes the meshes (KHR_mesh_quantization). That copy is unchanged from the pack but for what its row says. Nothing from Mixamo is added: its terms forbid handing out the raw files, which a web game sends to every browser.
